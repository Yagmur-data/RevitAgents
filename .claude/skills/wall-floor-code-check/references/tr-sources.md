## Corrections (things the official text contradicts or that are easy to get wrong)

1. **"Binalarda Isı Yalıtımı Yönetmeliği" is not in force.** BEP Madde 28 repealed it ("…Binalarda Isı Yalıtım Yönetmeliği yürürlükten kaldırılmıştır."). The current legal hook is BEP Art. 8–10 + TS 825. Any U-value table attributed to that regulation is obsolete. The skill should name the TR source "BEP Yönetmeliği + TS 825", not "Isı Yalıtımı Yönetmeliği". (Note: the wall-floor-code-check skill description currently says "Binalarda Isı Yalıtımı Yönetmeliği / TS 825".)
2. **IMAR Art. 28 storey heights (3.60 / 4.00 / 4.50 / 5.50 m) are MAXIMUMS** ("en fazla", top of slab to top of slab), not minimums. The minimums are clear heights: 2.60 m (habitable), 2.40 m (mezzanine, or air-navigation obstacle), 2.20 m (wet areas, passages, non-habitable basements, garages and plant). Since RG 12/8/2023, residential buildings of no more than 3 storeys (basement excluded) may go to 4.00 m.
3. **The guard height is 1.10 m from the floor level.** A guard is required at free edges of balconies, terraces, galleries and landings with no minimum drop. The numeric triggers are: open stairs or ramps with more than 1 step, gardens more than 0.50 m above or below, and window sills under 0.90 m. Max gap is 0.10 m. A "0.90 m sill" is a trigger, not a guard height.
4. **Fire wall vs compartment wall**: the 90 min figure applies to fire walls between bitişik nizam buildings (25(1)). Compartment walls are REI 60 minimum (definition tt; Ek-3/B row 6) or the Ek-3/C value if higher. 120 min belongs to escape-stair and fire-lobby walls (38(3), 34(2)), high-rise service shafts (25(3)), protected shafts (Ek-3/B row 8) and boiler rooms (54(2)). It is not a general "yangın duvarı" value.
5. **Ordinary lift shafts are 60 min** (62(2)). Only emergency-lift shafts take REI 120 (Ek-3/B row 9a). Landing doors are E 30, or E 60 above 51.50 m.
6. **Spandrel = 100 cm** of fire-resistant solid façade between unprotected openings of consecutive storeys, or a façade sprinkler line instead (27(1)). For ETICS over 6.50 m the extra barrier bands are 15 cm at the sides and 30 cm at the head (27(2)(b)).
7. **Noise regulation**: the limits are field DnT,A (= DnT,w + C) and L'nT,w at class C for new buildings (A/B only if the owner targets them, which also triggers a mandatory acoustic project per Art. 5(6)). A door may lower a partition by at most 14 dB (amended 2018). Do not check model wall types against "Rw ≥ 52": the 52 dB is DnT,A between dwellings, a field value that includes flanking.
8. **TBDY perde minimum is 250 mm and h/16** (7.6.1.2(a)). The 200 mm value applies only to shear-wall-dominant buildings meeting both Eq. (7.14) conditions (7.6.1.3), and tall buildings need 300 mm (13.1.6). Figures from the 2007 earthquake regulation are repealed (TBDY Art. 4).
9. **TBDY does not set solid-slab thickness**; it defers to TS 500. Do not cite TBDY for a "minimum plak döşeme" figure other than dişli 70 mm, masonry-building slab 100 mm and precast topping 70 mm.
10. **Ramp slope is not a single "8 %" rule.** IMAR 30(12) grades it by height climbed: 8 / 7 / 6 / 5 %. Escape ramps (YANGIN 44) are capped at 10 %, which is a fire-escape rule, not an accessibility one.
